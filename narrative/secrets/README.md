# 🧩 Secrets

## Overview

Secrets are facts about the world that are not known beyond a particular group and/or set of individuals. They typically add context or detail to existing areas of knowledge, and may be factual, subjective, intersubjective, incomplete, or even false. Secrets can be known in part or whole, or as differing perspectives on the same subject.
