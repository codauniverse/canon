# 🟦 Alpha-01

## Overview

Alpha-01 were the first elite fighting force formed from subjects of the Atlan Research Corps' [Clear Program](../../world/gata/military-and-defense/mavs.md#the-clear-serum-program), and the precursor to the [Unassisted Decisive Assets](../../world/gata/military-and-defense/mavs.md#unassisted-decisive-assets). The best of the best young cadets from JAC training camps around the world.
