# CAVE Defense

## Overview

A defense contractor specializing in research and development in material sciences, defensive solutions, performance telemetry, and they are a category-leading provider of personal armor gear, [frame cradles](../../science-and-tech/frames.md#design-and-functionality), and high-resilience plating for on-and-off-world applications.

CAVE is known for its long-standing partnership with [Angelis](../military-and-defense/angelis.md), tracing its origins to a [Pre-New Dawn](../history/new-dawn-declaration.md) military manufacturing compartment operated by the original [Research Corps](../../sol/institutions/the-research-corps.md). They provide a wide range of [personal combat gear](../../science-and-tech/gear.md#combat-gear) for the [MAVERICK](../military-and-defense/mavs.md#the-maverick-division) and [Rapid Response](../military-and-defense/rapid-response.md) divisions, and cradles for smaller [Guardian](../military-and-defense/guardians.md) [warframes](../../science-and-tech/frames.md#military).
